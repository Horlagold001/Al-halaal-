Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Al Halaal Sunamedic Therapy, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Your full name.
  • Phone number and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Email address, where provided.
  • Location, where provided.
  • Appointment and product enquiry details.
  • Messages submitted through our contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and questions.
  • Manage appointment requests.
  • Provide information about our services.
  • Respond to product enquiries.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our website may provide links to WhatsApp for convenient communication. When you contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

4. Protection of Information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate our website or respond to your requests, or where required by law.

6. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to external websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests concerning your personal information, please contact us.
