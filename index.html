About Al Halaal Sunamedic Therapy


Al Halaal Sunamedic Therapy is a wellness and Ruqyah service based in Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria, founded by Abdulbaasit Adeyemi. Our aim is to provide a welcoming environment where individuals can access information, seek consultations and explore selected wellness products through an approach guided by our understanding of the Sunnah.

We understand that people have different concerns and questions when seeking wellness support. That is why we place importance on listening, treating every individual with respect and providing a comfortable space for enquiries and consultations. Whether you are visiting us for a Ruqyah consultation, seeking information about our services or making enquiries about our available products, we are committed to giving you a courteous and helpful experience.

Our services include Ruqyah consultations for people with spiritual concerns, available through office visits and online appointments. We also offer selected products, with details and availability provided upon enquiry.

At Al Halaal Sunamedic Therapy, we believe that good service begins with respect, sincerity and clear communication. We welcome enquiries from individuals who wish to learn more about our approach, services and products.

Our Commitment: To serve with respect, maintain a welcoming atmosphere and provide clear information to everyone who reaches out to us.

Al Halaal Sunamedic Therapy was founded by Abdulbaasit Adeyemi with the aim of providing a welcoming space for people seeking wellness information, Ruqyah consultations and selected products through a Sunnah-oriented approach.
